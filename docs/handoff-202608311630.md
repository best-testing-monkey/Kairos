### 2. Parallel sweep driver

`scripts/run_model_parallel.py` (new). Measured GPU utilisation during a sweep at
**28.8% mean** — i.e. ~71% of wall clock is non-GPU (fetch, GIL-bound per-day
strategy loops). Baz's suggestion: serialise GPU work, parallelise the rest.

- **Phase 1** (GPU) prewarms `kairos_predcache`; **phase 2** (CPU pool) replays via
  `run_stage_model(..., extra_env={"KAIROS_PRED_CACHE_DIR": ...})`, the mechanism
  `--stage auto` already used.
- **Cache-key parity is the whole design risk**, and it is *proven*, not assumed: a
  real replay after a prewarm logged **zero** `Loading Kronos model` lines while
  still ranking 34 strategies. A unit test drives the real `KairosOrchestrator`
  with a stub predictor and asserts the enumeration matches exactly.
- Chunked (default 16) because the predcache has a 2GiB LRU disk budget — a global
  prewarm could evict its own entries before replay reads them.
- Later added `--pipeline` (overlaps chunk N+1's prewarm with chunk N's replay) and
  `--gpu-workers auto` (sizes per chunk from free VRAM **and** RAM, self-calibrating
  from observed peaks, logging which term bound the decision).

Measured: chunk 1 at 51.3s/group vs ~110s single-worker (**2.1×**); later chunks
32.2s/group. Full suite green throughout (1805 → 1816 → 1827 passed).

### 3. Comparison tooling and the actual result

`scripts/compare_model_stages.py` (new) enforces three rules in code rather than
leaving them to the caller: current-exit-rule rows only, the seven
`trend_following` aliases collapsed, and inner-join pairing so stages with
different coverage can't be silently compared.

**Result — small vs base, 180 paired groups, 1d/6m:**

| class | groups | base | small | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **equity** | **170** | **+0.544** | **+0.564** | **parity, well-powered** |
| crypto | 11 | +2.301 | +2.149 | underpowered |
| fx_commodity | 3 | +0.517 | +0.775 | uninterpretable |

Pooled: oracle +2.809 / small +0.113 / base 0.000 / naive −0.321.

Two findings that matter more than the headline:

- **Aggregate parity hides per-strategy divergence.** Within equity, strategies
  flip sign between models — `rl_meta_controller` +0.25→−0.49 (n=50),
  `hurst_regime_switch` −0.35→+0.30 (n=34). **Adopting small would require
  recomputing the disabled-strategy set, not inheriting base's.**
- **VRAM does not scale with parameter count.** Measured directly: mini's weights
  are 31 MiB and `max_context` contributes ~7 MiB (512 vs 2048). The 790 MiB floor
  is CUDA context + activations. Mini is only 13% lighter than small despite being
  6× smaller, so **mini's case must rest on speed and quality, not memory.**
